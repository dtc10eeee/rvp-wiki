7. Modular Damage ​

RVP resolves damage by part and module; different parts have independent durability, damage multipliers, and failure effects.

Part/moduleFailure effect
ERAThe hit block fails after being penetrated, loses its protection, and changes appearance
EngineSevere damage greatly reduces power and halves top speed; complete destruction removes all power and mobility
BarrelSevere damage may cause firing failures or a barrel burst; even successful shots lose accuracy. Complete destruction prevents firing. At stage-1 damage, missile-type weapons only succeed or fail; they do not cause a barrel burst
TraverseSevere damage greatly reduces traverse speed; complete destruction prevents rotation
Fire control system (FCS)Stabilizer fails, ballistic calculation and CCIP stop, thermal/night vision and EO search become unavailable; the crosshair remains but provides no valid ballistic solution
RadarDestroyed radar cannot search or track targets
APS/jammer/DIRCM/countermeasure launcherDestroyed equipment loses its function
Maintenance equipmentDestroyed maintenance equipment cannot perform quick repairs

Damage calculation uses part OBBs and independent damage multipliers; explosion direction, impact angle, armor reduction parameters, distance, ammunition type, and ERA state all affect final damage.

The repair panel restores parts by priority; ERA and support-equipment repair orders can be set separately.
