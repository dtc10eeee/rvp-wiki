## Quick Start

1. Install Minecraft 1.20.1 + Forge 47.4.x.
2. Place `ywzj_vehicle-1.20.1-0.6.2.1.jar` and `ywzj_rvp-1.20.1-1.0.0_preview_0.6.2.1-full.jar` into `mods/`.
3. Launch the client or server once and wait for the vehicle pack to be extracted to `limitless_vehicle/rvp/`.
4. Confirm that RVP vehicles and items appear in the creative inventory.

Full download, requirements, and feedback instructions are on the [Release page](https://github.com/TV90/limitless-vehicle-rvp-addon/releases/tag/1.0.0_preview_0.6.2.1).
